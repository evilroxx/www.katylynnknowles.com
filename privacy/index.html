Privacy

Effective 2026-11-22

Who runs this site

This site is run by Katy Lynn Knowles, a theatre educator, director and choreographer based in New York and Sarasota.

What is collected

If you accept analytics cookies in the banner, two services collect usage data about your visit:

  • Google Analytics (through Google Tag Manager) records usage statistics such as the pages you view, how long you stay, your approximate location and your browser and device type.
  • Microsoft Clarity records session replays and heatmaps showing how visitors scroll, click and move through the pages. Text typed into form fields is masked.

If you decline, neither service sets analytics cookies or records your visit.

The contact form on the home page does not send or store what you type. If you email me, I receive your email address and your message.

How consent works

A banner asks for your choice the first time you visit. Your choice is saved in your browser. You can change it at any time with the "Cookie settings" link in the page footer.

What we do not do

Your data is not sold and is not used for advertising. Advertising storage and ad personalization stay switched off.

Who processes the data

  • Google: Google Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft: Microsoft Privacy Statement

How long it is kept

Analytics and recording data is kept according to the retention settings of Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity. Google Analytics defaults to 14 months and Clarity keeps recordings for about 13 months.

Questions or requests about your data: katyknowles@hotmail.com.
